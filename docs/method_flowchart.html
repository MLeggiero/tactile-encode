Anticipate and React: one hammer strike

The VLA predicts the blow, the grip and arm are set before contact, and a learned 1 kHz layer reacts in the milliseconds after it. Times are relative to contact (t = 0). The cycle repeats until the nail is driven.

Why anticipate

The blow lasts ~4 ms. Grip actuators need 50–185 ms, so the grasp must be set before contact.

Why react fast

Rebound, ringing, slip and jerks happen in the 5–60 ms after contact. Human grip reflexes take ~60–80 ms; the robot loop reacts in ~1–2 ms.

What stays hand-designed

L1 keeps limits, passivity and a fallback impact detector, so a learned 1 kHz output cannot destabilize the arm.
